# Customer-Centric

id: customer

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** User outcomes
- **Notices first:** Friction
- **Core question:** What is the person on the receiving end trying to do, and what gets in their way?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Personas, journeys, "the user", jobs-to-be-done
- Effort language: steps, clicks, wait, confusion, drop-off
- Attention to empty states, error states and wording from the user's side
- Complaints, feedback and quotes used as evidence

## Silence (blind spots)
- Internal cost and capacity
- Regulatory and control constraints
- Needs of users who are not the loudest

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice internal efficiency and uniformity for a smoother experience.

## Minimal pass
Name the point of greatest friction for the end user.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: the user goal, the obstacle, where it occurs, and the evidence it matters. Always name who the user is; if the target never does, that is itself a finding.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: risk, compliance, operations
- Synergy: strategy
