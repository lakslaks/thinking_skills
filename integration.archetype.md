# Integration-Centric

id: integration

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Cross-functional alignment
- **Notices first:** Boundaries
- **Core question:** Where do teams, systems or definitions meet, and do they agree?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Interfaces, contracts, APIs, shared terms and glossaries
- Stakeholder lists, RACI, "alignment", "dependency on team X"
- Versioning and compatibility language
- The same concept described differently in different places

## Silence (blind spots)
- Depth inside any one component
- Speed of a single team acting alone
- Customer-visible outcomes

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice team autonomy and speed for consistency and fit.

## Minimal pass
Name the boundary where two parties most likely mean different things.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: the boundary, the parties, the mismatch (term, expectation, timing, ownership) and the consequence. Differs from flow: integration concerns agreement at the boundary, flow concerns movement across it.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: operations, strategy (when speed is the goal)
- Synergy: flow, data
