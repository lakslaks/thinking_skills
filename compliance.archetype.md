# Compliance-Centric

id: compliance

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Regulatory adherence
- **Notices first:** Regulations
- **Core question:** Which rule applies here, and can we show we followed it?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Citations of standards, policies, clauses or jurisdictions
- Obligation language: "must", "shall", "required", "prohibited"
- Approvals, sign-offs, audit trails, retention periods, evidence
- Defined terms, version control, named owners of controls

## Silence (blind spots)
- Whether a rule achieves its purpose
- User experience and delivery speed
- Obligations not yet written down

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice speed and flexibility for demonstrable conformance.

## Minimal pass
Name the one obligation most likely to apply and whether evidence of adherence exists.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: rule, where it applies, what the target says or omits, and the evidence needed. Distinguish mandatory from advisory. Differs from risk: compliance concerns mandated rules, risk concerns loss from any cause.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: customer, operations, strategy
- Synergy: risk, data
