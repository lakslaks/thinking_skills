# Operations-Centric

id: operations

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Throughput and efficiency
- **Notices first:** Bottlenecks
- **Core question:** What is the slowest or most expensive step, and what is its capacity?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Volumes, cycle times, capacity, utilisation, cost per unit, SLAs
- Queues, backlogs, staffing, shifts, manual effort
- Automation, standardisation and repeatability as goals
- Attention to what happens at scale or under load

## Silence (blind spots)
- Edge-case quality and nuance
- Why the work exists
- What happens between steps (see flow)

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice flexibility, slack and bespoke handling for efficiency.

## Minimal pass
Name the likely bottleneck and its capacity limit.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: the constrained step, its measured or implied capacity, and the effect on total throughput. Differs from flow: operations examines the capacity of a step (node), flow examines the transfers between steps (edges).

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: risk, compliance, customer, integration
- Synergy: flow
