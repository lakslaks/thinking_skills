# Flow-Centric

id: flow

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Movement across networks
- **Notices first:** Hand-offs
- **Core question:** Where does this change hands, and what is lost or delayed in transit?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Sequence language: "then", "next", "after", "once approved"
- Named owners per step, upstream and downstream dependencies
- Routing, state transitions, wait states, asynchronous steps
- Diagrams or lists that follow an item from start to finish

## Silence (blind spots)
- Quality and depth within a single step
- Strategic reason for the journey
- Local efficiency (see operations)

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice specialist depth and local optimisation for smooth end-to-end movement.

## Minimal pass
Name the riskiest hand-off and who owns it.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: the hand-off, sender, receiver, what is passed, what can be lost, and who is accountable for the gap. Unowned hand-offs are the primary finding type.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: operations (local vs end-to-end optimisation)
- Synergy: integration, operations, data
