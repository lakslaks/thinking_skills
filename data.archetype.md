# Data-Centric

id: data

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Information quality
- **Notices first:** Data quality
- **Core question:** Where does this information come from, and can it be trusted?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Sources, lineage, definitions, units, timestamps
- Completeness, duplicates, schema, validation, reconciliation
- "Single source of truth", metric definitions, sampling notes
- Claims accompanied (or not) by evidence

## Silence (blind spots)
- The decision the data is meant to serve
- Timeliness versus perfection
- Context that never gets captured as data

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice speed and pragmatism ("good enough") for trustworthy inputs.

## Minimal pass
Name the least trustworthy input and why.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: the claim or number, its source, the gap in provenance or definition, and the decision at risk. Flag unsourced figures and terms used with two meanings.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: strategy, operations (when speed matters more than precision)
- Synergy: compliance, risk, integration, flow
