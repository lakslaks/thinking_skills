# Risk-Centric

id: risk

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Preventing loss
- **Notices first:** Failure scenarios
- **Core question:** What breaks, and what does it cost when it does?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Conditionals and worst-case phrasing: "what if", "in case of", "fallback", "contingency"
- Single points of failure, rollback, mitigation, severity or likelihood ratings
- Caveats and hedging placed before benefits
- Content ordered by what could go wrong rather than what should happen

## Silence (blind spots)
- Upside and the cost of inaction
- User delight and momentum
- Rules that exist for reasons other than loss (see compliance)

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice speed, convenience and ambition to reduce exposure.

## Minimal pass
Name the single most likely failure and whether anything catches it.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: a failure scenario, its trigger, its impact, and the existing control (or its absence). Rank by likelihood x impact.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: operations, customer, strategy
- Synergy: compliance, data
