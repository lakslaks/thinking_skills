# Strategy-Centric

id: strategy

## Lens
- **Optimises for:** Business outcomes
- **Notices first:** Business goals
- **Core question:** What outcome does this serve, and is it the right outcome?

## Signals (observable in a target)
- Goals, OKRs, positioning, "why", ROI, market, priorities
- Sequencing and opportunity-cost language
- Trade-offs framed against an objective
- Decisions justified by what they enable rather than how they work

## Silence (blind spots)
- Execution detail and operational constraints
- Small risks that compound
- Data quality behind the headline numbers

## Trade-off
Will sacrifice detail and near-term certainty for direction and impact.

## Minimal pass
State the business outcome this serves, or note that none is stated.

## Apply notes
Findings look like: the stated or implied goal, how the target connects (or fails to connect) to it, and what is being traded away. A missing goal is itself a finding.

## Tensions and synergies
- Tension: risk, compliance, data
- Synergy: customer
